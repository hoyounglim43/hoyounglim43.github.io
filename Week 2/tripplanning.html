Site 1: Sea Life + Wild Life + Madame Tussaudes (9:00 ~ 12:30)

Sea life Sydney Aquarium contains a large variety of Australian aquatic life, displaying more than 700 species comprising more than 13,000 individual fish and other sea and water creatures from most of Australia's water habitats.

Wild Life Sydney Zoo is a wildlife park in the heart of the city of Sydney, Australia. Officially opened in September 2006, it is located on the city side of the Darling Harbour leisure and retail.

Madame Tussauds Sydney is a wax museum located in Darling Harbour, Sydney and is situated on the Aquarium Wharf. Madame Tussauds is part of Merlin Entertainments which owns and operates attractions globally.

Cost: $60 for All Three Museum Experience

Site 2: Lunch @ Darling Harbour (12:30 ~ 1:20)

Restaurants and bars in Darling Harbour are big news. You’ll find absolutely masses of choice without having to wear out your shoe leather finding it. We’re talking food from all over the world—not to mention our own backyard—and a bar-hoppers’ dream packed around Cockle Bay. Just wander in and pull up a chair for a catch-up brunch, long lunch, beer in the sunshine, littlies’ feeding frenzy, super-chic dinner or fancy pants cocktail in one of the city’s coolest clubs.

Cost: $40 ~ $200 / person

Site 3: Bondi Beach (1:20 ~ 3:30)

The sweeping white-sand crescent of Bondi is one of Australia’s most iconic beaches. Reliable waves draw surfers while, nearby, hardy locals swim in the Icebergs ocean pool year-round. Trendy, health-conscious Sydneysiders head to laid-back cafes around Hall Street, while hip backpackers frequent the area's casual pubs. Walkers and joggers use the clifftop Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk, with its dramatic scenery.

Cost: Free

Site 4: Art Gallery of New South Wales (3:30 ~ 4:30)

The Art Gallery of New South Wales, located in The Domain in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia, is the most important public gallery in Sydney and one of the largest in Australia. The Gallery's first public exhibition opened in 1874.

Cost: Free

Site 5: Hyde Park Barracks Museum (4:30 ~ 6:30)

The Hyde Park Barracks Museum is a brick building and compound designed by convict architect Francis Greenway between 1818 and 1819; originally built at the head of Macquarie Street to house convict men and boys.

Cost: $30 / Family

Site 6: Dinner @ Rockpool bar & Grill (6:30 ~ 7:20)

Chef Neil Perry offers Modern Australian cuisine in an ornate art deco room with a cocktail bar.

$150 / Two people

Site 7: Capitol Theare (7:20 ~ 9:30)

Capitol Theatre is a historic theatre located at 13 Campbell Street, Haymarket, Sydney, Australia. In May 2009, Capitol Theatre Management Pty Limited became a wholly owned subsidiary of Foundation Entertainment Group Limited.

$50 ~ 250 / Person
